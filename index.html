About Company

A True Local Transportation Solution

Ascend Tour and Car Rental is a local supplier of transport solutions, mainly car rental services. Our offerings are designed to ensure client flexibility, comfort, and convenience.

If your business is not transport services, we are here to ensure your transport needs are met so you can focus on what you do best. Our repeat clients and growing customer base are testament to our commitment.

Our Vision

To become Ethiopia's leading tour and car rental company, recognized for reliability, customer satisfaction, and excellence in service.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, safe, and high-quality travel and car rental services, prioritizing safety, efficiency, and customer satisfaction.
